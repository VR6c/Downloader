macOS FFmpeg & FFprobe Binaries
================================
These binaries are standalone, statically linked Universal 2 Mach-O executables
supporting both Apple Silicon (arm64 - M1/M2/M3/M4/A-series) and Intel (x86_64).

Source: https://ffmpeg.martin-riedl.de/
They contain no external Homebrew/Cellar dynamic library dependencies and are
automatically bundled into the application's resources/bin/mac folder by electron-builder.
